# Site text audit: CMO diesel metatranscriptomes (2026-10-23)

Scope: `scripts/site_text.py` (FINDINGS, CAVEATS) and the kicker paragraphs and `fig()` captions in `site/app.js`.
Each claim was recomputed from local data (`site/*.json`, `out/`, `data/`, `cyclo/`, `hgt/`). Audit scripts are `audit/a*.py` and `audit/perm_check.py`, all run with `python3 -I`.

Verdicts: **OK** = confirmed; **MINOR** = rounding, wording or scope slightly off, with corrected text; **WRONG** = wrong or not supported by the data; **OVERREACH** = the numbers are right but the inference goes beyond what one pool per treatment can support; **n/v** = could not be checked locally.

## Conventions found while recomputing

- The hydrocarbon family CPMs on the site (`data.json → pathways.families[*].cpm`) match `out/hc_cpm.tsv` to within 0.005. They also match sums of the coordinate-remapped `data/ko_cpm.tsv` (r = 1.000), so the KO misalignment does not affect them.
- **The fold changes in the FINDINGS text are ratios of medians with a +0.1 pseudocount** (as in `scripts/hc.py`). The degradation-map figure shows **ratios of means, +0.5 pseudocount, seawater and ice pooled**. So the text and the figure give different numbers for the same enzyme. For example, toluene monooxygenase in 2025-26 is 43× in the text, 28× as a seawater mean, and 18× in the map box.
- "% of mapped transcripts" for Cycloclasticus (`cyclo.json → mag_timecourse`) is really a percentage of **CDS-assigned read pairs** (the denominator is `pairs_cds` = sum of `bin_counts`), not of all mapped pairs.
- The DNA percentages differ by source. `out/dna_rel_abundance.tsv` gives **% of binned coverage**; `cyclo.json → dna` gives **% of mapped bases**. The site mixes them.
- The 2024-25 "pre" group has n = 2 per pool, and the diesel pool's pair includes CMO_EXP_DAY05_SW, which the site itself flags as a probable label swap: its RNA and DNA look like the control. 2025-26 has five pre-dosing samples per pool, all seawater. Neither season has pre-dosing ice.

## Claims that overreach pseudoreplicated, one-pool-per-arm data

1. **"Pulls away within days of dosing"** (community kicker). The diesel pool was already distinct before dosing in both winters. This is a pool effect until shown otherwise.
2. **Fold changes stated as diesel effects** (F3, F5b, degradation map). They are between-pool ratios, and in 2025-26 the diesel pool already had about 2–2.5× the control's aromatic transcription before dosing. Quote BACI ratios (11–14× for the 2025-26 aromatics) next to, or instead of, the raw ratios.
3. **Sulfurimonas "switched on" by diesel** (F4, markers kicker). In 2025-26 seawater the diesel pool did not change at dosing; the control declined. In 2024-25 the diesel pool started 6× higher. The data show a pool difference that persists. They do not show diesel turning this metabolism on.
4. **Marine oil snow "hotspot"** (F6). Two unlabelled samples, compared against early seawater. Contemporaneous seawater is within about 3×.
5. **"Diesel suppresses animals and photosynthesis"** (F8 header) and the eukaryote declines. These are single-pool contrasts. In 2025-26 the logs also record the roofs differing between pools (diesel pool open, control closed) on pre-dosing days, so light differed as well as treatment.
6. **Ice vs seawater differences "within the oiled pool"** (F6c) and the cytochrome bd low-oxygen inference. There is no pre-dosing ice in either winter, so these cannot be BACI-corrected.
7. **p-values and q-values** in the MAG explorer (q < 0.05) and in the KO consistency counts treat time points as replicates. C1 says this, but the MAG explorer kicker uses q < 0.05 as a selection criterion without that warning.
8. **"Two different Cycloclasticus species bloomed in the two winters"** (F2). This is well supported as a description. Explanations (carry-over, colonisation) are rightly left open; keep them that way.
